# Forge-Theory-Suite — Sovereign Multi-Layer Research Ecosystem
> A unified architecture spanning AI, robotics, trading systems, simulation, emergence, and computational theory.

Forge-Theory-Suite is the canonical root of the Forge-Theory-Labs ecosystem — a multi-domain research environment containing 7+ engineering layers, 40+ engines, and 8+ years of experimental development across intelligence, robotics, simulation, trading, emergence, and computational theory.

This repository is not a single project.
It is an ecosystem.

It contains:

- **forge-theory** — the full family of computational forges
- **intelligence** — AI engines, frameworks, and experimental models
- **robotics** — embedded systems, emulators, and control logic
- **trading** — market engines, CCE systems, and quantitative tooling
- **ubvm** — the Unified Biological Virtual Machine
- **archives** — historical experiments, SCP capsules, and research artifacts
- **utilities** — operational scripts, wires, and tooling
- **cognitive & simulation** — emergence, language, ecosystems, and world-models

This suite is the sovereign index of the entire Forge-Theory-Labs organisation.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy

Forge-Theory-Suite is built on three principles:

**1. Multi-Domain Interoperability**
Systems from robotics, AI, trading, and simulation share common abstractions.

**2. Emergence from Simple Rules**
Complex behaviour arises from small, composable engines.

**3. Ecosystem Sovereignty**
Every layer is self-contained, recoverable, and operationally independent.

This is not a monolith — it is a living ecosystem.
