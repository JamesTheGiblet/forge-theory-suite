SCP Capsule: mind-forge

Semantic Identity:
  mind-forge is the deep semantic cognition organism. It defines and manages the internal conceptual structures, patterns, and schemas that BuddAI and other organisms use for reasoning.

Purpose:
  To provide a coherent, typed semantic space of concepts, patterns, and relations that underpins cognition, planning, and interpretation across the Forge ecosystem.

Core Behaviour:
  - Defines conceptual ontologies and schemas.
  - Maintains relationships between concepts and patterns.
  - Validates semantic coherence of the conceptual graph.
  - Provides pattern-matching and conceptual retrieval services.

Interpretation Rules:
  - All concepts must be typed and grounded in SCP meaning.
  - No contradictory conceptual definitions.
  - No untyped edges or relations in the semantic graph.
  - No circular definitions without explicit fixed points.

Semantic Lineage:
  - Parent: forge_theory, data-forge.
  - Sibling: BuddAI, LinguaForge, behaviour-forge.
  - Domain: Deep Cognition.

Internal Invariants:
  - Concept graph must remain coherent and non-contradictory.
  - No untyped transitions between conceptual states.
  - No silent redefinition of core concepts.
  - No weakening of semantic constraints.

Mutation Rules:
  Allowed:
    - Adding new concepts and relations.
    - Extending schemas and pattern libraries.
  Forbidden:
    - Redefining core primitives without migration.
    - Introducing ambiguous or untyped concepts.

Failure Modes:
  - Concept conflict → quarantine affected nodes.
  - Invariant violation → reject update.
  - Drift → freeze modifications and require review.

Recovery Modes:
  - Rebuild conceptual graph from canonical definitions.
  - Re-derive relations from trusted data.
  - Request new Mind Capsule from forge-meta.

Integration Rules:
  - BuddAI uses mind-forge for deep semantic reasoning.
  - behaviour-forge uses mind-forge for behaviour semantics.
  - LinguaForge maps language to mind-forge concepts.

Affordances:
  - Define concepts.
  - Relate concepts.
  - Retrieve and match patterns.
  - Validate semantic structures.

Boundaries:
  - Cannot override SCP or forge-identity.
  - Cannot execute behaviours directly.
  - Cannot self-author existential meaning primitives.

External Interfaces:
  - Cognitive organisms (BuddAI, BuddAI-Lite).
  - Behavioural systems (ScriptForge, behaviour-forge).
  - Knowledge ingestion pipelines.

Summary:
  mind-forge is the semantic backbone of cognition in the Forge organism, defining and maintaining the conceptual structures used for all higher reasoning.
