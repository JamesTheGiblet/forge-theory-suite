## Part A — How UK Copyright Works for Software

### The Core Fact
In the United Kingdom, **copyright arises automatically** at the moment of creation. There is no registration system, no fee, and no application required. Under the **Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (CDPA 1988)**, original literary works — which include computer programs, source code, specifications, and technical documentation — are protected from the instant they are recorded in material form.

You do not need to register anything. UBVM and SCP are already protected.

### What Is Covered
The following works are protected under CDPA 1988 s.3:

- All source code (Node.js runtime, dispatcher, event bus, capsule executor)
- The SCP JSON schema and capsule format specification
- The UBVM whitepaper and technical specification documents
- The Meaning Sovereignty Licence v1.0 text
- All README files, documentation, and architectural diagrams
- The SCP Super Plugin VS Code extension source
- Any original naming conventions, terminology, and conceptual frameworks

### Duration
Copyright in a computer program lasts for **70 years from the end of the calendar year in which the author dies** (CDPA 1988 s.12, as amended by the Duration of Copyright Regulations 1995).

### Your Rights as Author
As the sole author and creator, you hold:
- The right to copy the work (s.16)
- The right to issue copies to the public (s.18)
- The right to communicate the work to the public (s.20)
- The right to make adaptations (s.21)
- **Moral rights**: the right to be identified as author (s.77) and the right to object to derogatory treatment (s.80)

> **Important:** Moral rights under s.77 must be **asserted** — they are not automatic. The standard method is a written assertion, which this Declaration provides.
