WHAT THIS IS

The Operator will configure and run the CCE (Cascade Compounding Engine)
— an autonomous algorithmic trading platform — against the Client's
personal Kraken exchange account. The Client retains full ownership and
custody of their funds at all times. The Operator has trade-only API
access and cannot withdraw funds.

This is not a regulated investment product. This is a private technology
service arrangement between individuals who understand and accept the
risks involved.

---

TERMS

1. SETUP FEE
A one-time setup fee of £200 is payable before configuration begins.
This covers platform setup, API configuration, Telegram integration,
and ongoing technical support. This fee is non-refundable once setup
is complete.

2. ONGOING FEE
The platform automatically siphons a percentage of net profitable trades
to the Operator via the CSS (Capital Siphon System). The siphon rate is
tiered based on total return performance:

  - 0% to 50% total return:    10% siphon (Client keeps 90%)
  - 50% to 100% total return:  12% siphon (Client keeps 88%)
  - Above 100% total return:   15% siphon (Client keeps 85%)

Siphon is deducted automatically — no manual invoicing. No fees are
charged on losing trades or during DORMANT periods. The siphon rate
is calculated monthly based on the Client's total return since inception.

3. CLIENT FUNDS
The Client's capital is held entirely within their own Kraken account.
The Operator has API access with trade permission only — withdrawals
are technically impossible via the API connection. The Client may log
in to Kraken at any time to view their balance, cancel orders, or
withdraw funds.

4. PLATFORM OPERATION
The CCE platform operates autonomously 24/7. It may remain in DORMANT
state for extended periods — this is normal behaviour during unfavourable
market conditions. The Operator does not manually intervene in trading
decisions.

5. REPORTING
The Client will receive:
- Monthly portfolio update via Telegram
- Real-time trade notifications via Telegram
- Access to their balance on Kraken at any time
- Notification of significant events (circuit breaker triggered,
  engine state changes)

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement at any time with 7 days
written notice. On termination, the Operator removes API access and
the Client retains full control of their Kraken account and all
remaining funds.

7. RISKS
The Client confirms they understand:
- Cryptocurrency trading carries significant risk including total
  loss of capital
- Past performance does not guarantee future results
- The platform may be DORMANT for weeks or months — this is correct
  behaviour
- The Operator provides a technology service and is not a financial
  advisor
- This is not FCA regulated activity
- The Client is responsible for their own tax obligations on profits
