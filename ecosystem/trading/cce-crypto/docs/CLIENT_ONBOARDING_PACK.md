PART 1 — WHAT IS CCE?
================================================================================

CCE (Cascade Compounding Engine) is an autonomous algorithmic trading
platform. It runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, watching the market and
making trading decisions based on a set of rules — no emotion, no guessing,
no manual intervention.

Your money stays in your own Kraken account at all times. CCE connects to
your account via an API key — think of it like giving a trusted accountant
read-and-trade access to your account, while keeping the ability to withdraw
entirely in your hands. CCE cannot withdraw your funds. Ever.

WHAT CCE DOES:

- Monitors Bitcoin price, market sentiment, and dominance 24/7
- Waits for the right conditions before making any trade
- Makes small, calculated trades when conditions align
- Exits positions when targets are hit or conditions change
- Reports every trade to you via Telegram

WHAT CCE DOES NOT DO:

- It does not guarantee profits
- It does not trade recklessly
- It does not have access to withdraw your funds
- It does not require you to do anything after setup

PART 6 — YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED
================================================================================

Q: Is my money safe?
A: Your funds are held in your own Kraken account. CCE has trade-only
   access — it cannot withdraw. You can revoke access at any time by
   deleting the API key in Kraken.

Q: What if I want my money back?
A: Log into Kraken, delete the API key, and withdraw your funds.
   CCE will stop trading automatically. No notice required.

Q: Is this regulated?
A: No. This is a private technology service between individuals, not
   an FCA-regulated investment product. You are responsible for your
   own tax obligations on any profits.

Q: What if CCE loses money?
A: All trading carries risk. CCE is designed to minimise losses through
   circuit breakers (automatic stop if daily loss exceeds 3%) and by
   staying DORMANT in hostile market conditions. There is no guarantee
   of profit and you could lose some or all of your capital.

Q: How do I know what CCE is doing?
A: You will receive Telegram notifications for every action. You also
   have full access to your Kraken account to see every trade in real
   time. Your dashboard shows all engine states and market conditions.

Q: Can I add more money later?
A: Yes. Just deposit more into your Kraken account and let James know
   so the starting capital figure can be updated.

Q: What is the minimum starting capital?
A: Recommended minimum is £500. Below this, trading fees eat into
   returns significantly.
