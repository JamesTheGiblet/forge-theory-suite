## Overview

GoldForge is a two-engine system designed to systematically accumulate
physical gold using profits generated by the CCE trading platform.

Where CSS siphons profits into a gold reserve fund, GoldForge decides
when and how to convert that fund into actual physical gold — coins,
bars, or allocated storage — based on macro regime signals.

---

## The Two Engines

### Goldfinger — Tactical Entry Engine
Watches the Gold/BTC ratio and spot gold price for optimal entry points.
Triggers buy signals when gold is statistically undervalued relative to
its recent range or when the macro regime strongly favours hard assets.
Goldfinger FSM:
DORMANT → WATCHING → ACCUMULATING → HOLDING → DORMANT
**Entry conditions:**
- Gold Z-score < -1.5 (statistically cheap)
- G.O regime = RISK_OFF for > 14 days
- DXY weakening (dollar losing strength)
- F&G < 25 (fear environment — gold safe haven bid)

### Goldmember — Strategic Allocation Engine
Manages the long-term gold/BTC reserve ratio. Determines what percentage
of total reserves should be in gold vs BTC based on macro cycle position.
Goldmember targets:
Early bull:   20% gold / 80% BTC
Mid bull:     30% gold / 70% BTC
Late bull:    50% gold / 50% BTC
Bear market:  70% gold / 30% BTC

## Purchase Mechanism

GoldForge does not automate the actual purchase — it generates buy signals
and sends Telegram alerts with:

- Amount to purchase (in grams or oz)
- Recommended supplier (Royal Mint, BullionVault, etc.)
- Current spot price and target entry
- Rationale (regime, Z-score, DXY trend)

Manual execution preserves full control over the physical purchase.
