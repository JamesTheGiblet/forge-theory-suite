## Governance Principles

### Semantic Clarity
Every component must have a clear identity and domain.  
Ambiguity is treated as a structural defect.

### Deterministic Evolution
Changes must preserve the integrity of the ecosystem’s architecture.  
No cross-domain contamination without explicit justification.

### Sovereign Autonomy
Each domain is self-contained, internally coherent, and governed by its own logic — yet all domains integrate into a unified whole.

## Domain-Level Governance
