# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 19, 2025

## Introduction

Your privacy is critically important to us. **Fluffy Bot / Cool Boy** (BuddAI Lite) is designed with a "Privacy-First" architecture. This means that, by default, your conversation data, projects, and preferences **never leave your device**.

## Data Collection & Storage

### 1. Local Data (Your Device)

We use your browser's **Local Storage** (`localStorage`) to save:

- Chat history
- User preferences (topics, skill level)
- Project details
- Application state (current mode, voice settings)

This data is stored **only on your device**. We (the developers) do not have access to this data. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache or using the "Reset Learning Data" button in the application.

### 2. Analytics (Simple Analytics)

We use **Simple Analytics** to understand website traffic. This service is privacy-friendly:

- 🚫 **No cookies** are used.
- 🚫 **No IP addresses** are stored.
- 🚫 **No personal data** is collected.
- ✅ We only see aggregate data (e.g., "100 visitors today").

You can read more about their privacy practices at simpleanalytics.com/privacy.

### 3. Voice Processing

Voice output is handled by your browser's built-in **Web Speech API**. No audio data is sent to our servers.

## Data Ownership

You own your data. We provide tools to:

- **Export** your data (JSON, TXT, HTML, Markdown).
- **Delete** your data (Clear Chat, Reset Preferences).

## Third-Party Services

- **GitHub Pages:** This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server logs (IP addresses) for security and reliability purposes. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be documented in our CHANGELOG.md.
