## 📜 The Journey – MK1 to MK20

### MK1 – CCE (Centralized Crypto Engine)
*The beginning.* – Basic trading bot on Kraken, simple RSI strategies.

### MK2 – GOLEM
*The mind awakens.* – AI agents, self‑healing, first autonomous decisions.

### MK3 – LEGION
*The vault is built.* – SCP‑style strategy vault, hash‑chained 3‑agent voting, JSON as law.

### MK4 – The Awakening
*The tournament begins.* – Paper capital competition, Treasurer, rankings.

### MK5 – Ascension
*AI judges the evolution.* – Chameleon LM, Telegram alerts, market regime router.

### MK6 – Sovereignty
*Real data, real markets.* – Kraken live prices (7 assets), Crypto VIX, Aegis three‑locks.

### MK7 – Transcendence
*The portfolio awakens.* – Portfolio allocator, Fear & Greed sentiment, cross‑asset arbitrage detection.

### MK8 – Apotheosis
*The dashboard.* – Web dashboard with live charts, settings, API keys, light/dark mode.

### MK9 – Genesis
*The machine learns to create.* – Genetic algorithm auto‑strategy generator, Forge Lord integration.

### MK10 – Deep Intelligence
*The mind transcends.* – DQN reinforcement learning, Reddit sentiment, on‑chain whale tracking.

### MK11 – Voice
*The machine speaks.* – Narrator (hourly market summaries), voice commands (“LEGION, what’s the market doing?”), personality (mood based on PnL).

### MK12 – Autonomous Improvement
*Self‑analysis, self‑improvement.* – Detects weaknesses, generates suggestions, applies changes without asking.

### MK13 – Implementation
*Acts on its own suggestions.* – Auto‑tunes mutation rate, position size, trailing stops based on performance.

### MK14 – Cross‑Exchange Arbitrage
*Five exchanges.* – Kraken, Coinbase, Gemini, Bitstamp, Bybit. Detects price differences >0.3%.

### MK15 – On‑Chain Intelligence
*Raw blockchain data.* – Tracks whale addresses, mempool transactions, exchange flows.

### MK16 – Social Sentiment Network
*Reads the crowd.* – Crypto news, Twitter trends, sentiment scoring.

### MK17 – Decentralised Execution
*On‑chain trading.* – Uniswap V3, Raydium (Solana), MEV protection, flash loan detection.

### MK18 – Swarm Mode
*Multi‑instance.* – Leader election, distributed strategy execution.

### MK19 – Predictive Intelligence
*Forecasts the market.* – Linear regression / neural network price predictions, macro indicators (CPI, interest rates).

### MK20 – Total Sovereignty
*Autonomous action, sovereign control.* – LEGION acts without permission, but you own the kill switch. Full audit trail, emergency close, daily sovereignty reports.

> *“LEGION doesn’t need your permission – but you own the kill switch.”*
