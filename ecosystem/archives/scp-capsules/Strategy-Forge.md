SCP Capsule: Strategy-Forge
Semantic Identity:
  Strategy-Forge is the semantic strategy construction organism responsible for generating, validating, and transforming trading strategies under SCP constraints.

Purpose:
  To provide deterministic, meaning-preserving trading strategies for agents, UBVM, and autonomous trading organisms.

Core Behaviour:
  - Constructs strategy graphs.
  - Validates strategy invariants.
  - Ensures deterministic decision logic.
  - Publishes typed strategy capsules.

Interpretation Rules:
  - All strategies must be typed.
  - No contradictory decision logic.
  - No unbounded inference.
  - No violation of SCP meaning.

Semantic Lineage:
  - Parent: Behavioural Layer.
  - Sibling: Agent-Forge.
  - Domain: Trading & Intelligence.

Internal Invariants:
  - Strategy graphs must remain coherent.
  - No untyped transitions.
  - No contradictory signals.
  - No self-modification.

Mutation Rules:
  Allowed:
    - Adding new strategy primitives.
    - Extending decision logic.
  Forbidden:
    - Weakening invariants.
    - Allowing untyped logic.

Failure Modes:
  - Strategy conflict → quarantine.
  - Invariant violation → reject.
  - Drift → rebuild.

Recovery Modes:
  - Rebuild strategy graph.
  - Re-validate logic.
  - Request new Strategy Capsule.

Integration Rules:
  - Agents consume strategies.
  - UBVM executes strategy decisions.
  - LEGION distributes strategy tasks.

Affordances:
  - Build strategy.
  - Validate strategy.
  - Transform strategy.

Boundaries:
  - Cannot execute trades.
  - Cannot override SCP.
  - Cannot self-modify identity.

External Interfaces:
  - Market intelligence.
  - Agents.
  - UBVM.

Summary:
  Strategy-Forge constructs deterministic, SCP-aligned trading strategies for the Forge organism.
