SCP Capsule: forge-identity
Semantic Identity:
  forge-identity is the semantic self-definition organism of the Forge universe. It defines the identity, boundaries, lineage, and existential constraints of the Forge organism.

Purpose:
  To maintain a coherent, deterministic, meaning-preserving identity across all semantic layers, organisms, and distributed nodes.

Core Behaviour:
  - Defines the identity of the Forge organism.
  - Maintains semantic lineage and ancestry.
  - Enforces identity invariants across all subsystems.
  - Prevents identity drift or fragmentation.

Interpretation Rules:
  - Identity must remain coherent across all nodes.
  - No organism may self-author identity.
  - No untyped identity transitions allowed.
  - Identity must remain SCP-aligned.

Semantic Lineage:
  - Parent: SCP.
  - Sibling: forge-meta, forge-protocol.
  - Domain: Identity Layer.

Internal Invariants:
  - Identity must remain stable.
  - No contradictory identity states.
  - No weakening of existential constraints.
  - No divergence across distributed nodes.

Mutation Rules:
  Allowed:
    - Extending identity descriptors.
    - Adding new lineage metadata.
  Forbidden:
    - Changing core identity.
    - Allowing untyped identity changes.

Failure Modes:
  - Identity conflict → quarantine.
  - Drift → halt.
  - Invariant violation → reject.

Recovery Modes:
  - Rebuild identity graph.
  - Re-sync from authoritative node.
  - Request new Identity Capsule.

Integration Rules:
  - All organisms reference forge-identity.
  - LEGION enforces identity consistency.
  - UBVM uses identity for execution context.

Affordances:
  - Define identity.
  - Validate identity.
  - Transform identity metadata.

Boundaries:
  - Cannot override SCP.
  - Cannot self-modify identity.

External Interfaces:
  - All semantic organisms.

Summary:
  forge-identity defines and preserves the self of the Forge organism across all layers and nodes.
