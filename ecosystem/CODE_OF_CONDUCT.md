# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We commit to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment.

## Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the ecosystem.

## Unacceptable Behaviour
- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Disruptive or hostile behaviour.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in warnings, temporary restrictions, or removal from participation.
